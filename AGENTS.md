# PRESDA editorial rule

NEVER use em dashes (Unicode U+2014) in PRESDA editorial content.

This applies to article titles, subtitles, excerpts, body copy, headings, captions,
cards, SEO titles, meta descriptions, FAQs and editorial source labels.

Use natural sentence structure and appropriate commas, periods, colons,
semicolons, parentheses or conjunctions. Review each sentence individually;
do not apply a blanket punctuation substitution. Do not alter quotations to
silently misrepresent a source: paraphrase with attribution when necessary.

Preserve URLs, citations, facts and meaning. This rule does not authorize changes
to code syntax or unrelated UI. Check new or edited editorial content for both
literal and encoded em dashes before publishing.
