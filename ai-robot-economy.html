AI Assistants Move From Chat To Real Work

May 19, 2026TechnologyPRESDA Editorial

Artificial intelligence is moving beyond simple chat windows and into daily business operations, where assistants can summarize documents, prepare reports, organize research, and support customer workflows.

Companies are now testing AI systems as practical teammates rather than experimental tools. The focus is shifting from novelty to reliability, speed, and measurable productivity.

The next AI race is not only about smarter models. It is about useful work.
