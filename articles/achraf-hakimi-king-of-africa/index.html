King Of Africa: Achraf Hakimi Reaches 19 Major Trophies

With another historic European night, Achraf Hakimi has strengthened his place among the most decorated African footballers of the modern era.

Jun 24, 2026 PRESDA Editorial 4 MIN READ PRESDA Sport Coverage

Achraf Hakimi has built a career defined by speed, intelligence and decisive moments. From Madrid to Dortmund, Milan and Paris, the Moroccan full-back has continued to collect trophies while becoming one of Africa's most recognizable football figures.

His latest European success pushes his major trophy count to 19, a number that reflects more than silverware. It reflects consistency at elite clubs, the ability to perform under pressure and a rare capacity to shape matches from a position that once received less global attention.

For Morocco, Hakimi represents a new football identity. He is technical, modern, fearless and comfortable on the biggest stages. His rise arrived alongside Morocco's historic World Cup run, helping turn the Atlas Lions into one of the most respected teams in international football.

The modern full-back is no longer a supporting role. In Hakimi's case, it is a weapon.

What separates Hakimi is the balance between athletic power and tactical clarity. He can defend wide spaces, attack with precision and arrive in the final third like a winger. In modern football, that combination is priceless.

As the next World Cup approaches, Hakimi enters the tournament not as a prospect, but as a leader. His career already carries the weight of major trophies, but his international story with Morocco may still have its most emotional chapter ahead.
