WE ARE ALL MOROCCANS

The oldest known Homo sapiens ever discovered was found in Morocco.

Jun 6, 2026 PRESDA Editorial 3 MIN READ Nature, Max Planck Institute, Reuters
Filed Under Science Nature, Max Planck Institute, Reuters
X Facebook LinkedIn

At Jebel Irhoud, Morocco, scientists uncovered fossils that pushed the story of Homo sapiens much further back than previously believed.

The remains, dated to around 315,000 years ago, are widely considered among the oldest known Homo sapiens fossils ever discovered.

The discovery challenged the old idea that modern humans emerged from one single region of East Africa. Instead, it suggests that early Homo sapiens evolved across different parts of Africa, with Morocco playing a central role in that story.

Found alongside stone tools and evidence of early human activity, Jebel Irhoud became one of the most important archaeological sites in the world.

For Morocco, this is more than a scientific discovery. It is a reminder that the country holds one of the deepest chapters in the human story.

In a way, the headline says it all: we are all Moroccans.
