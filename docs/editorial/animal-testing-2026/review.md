# Animal testing editorial and publication checks

Reviewed 16 October 2026. Article: animal-testing-hidden-cost-scientific-progress. Four complete editions, EN/FR/AR/ES; 39 aligned content blocks and 24 linked sources in each edition. Original English feature is approximately 2,100 words excluding source links. No fabricated interviews or quotes.

## Evidence decisions

- Historical global estimates are explicitly labeled 2015, modeled rather than a 2026 census. 79.9 million procedural-use estimate and 192.1 million broader estimate are not interchangeable. Author affiliation and extrapolation limitations are disclosed.
- European Commission 2026 report: 7.97 million animals used for the first time in EU procedures in 2023, 4.9% below 2022. US USDA reporting exclusions prevent treating that series as a national all-species total.
- Insulin and polio examples credit multiple methods and contributors. Clinical failure rates are not presented as direct measures of animal-model accuracy.
- FDA April 2025 roadmap, March 2026 draft guidance and April 2026 progress report distinguished from binding rules and demonstrated outcomes.
- September 22, 2026 direct final rule: terminology changes, no new requirements, scheduled effective February 4, 2027 subject to comments/withdrawal. Not yet effective at review, not a universal ban.
- Cosmetics bans distinguished from REACH worker/environment obligations and US law. No claim of uniform global regulation.
- 3Rs, organoids, chips and AI presented with validation requirements and limitations. No unsupported date for complete replacement.
