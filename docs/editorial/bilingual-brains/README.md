## Evidence decisions

- Adult executive control: Lehtonen et al. (2018), 152 studies and 891 effect sizes. Distinguish uncorrected small differences from results after bias correction. Do not generalize a universal advantage.
- Childhood: distinguish code mixing from confusion, and bilingual development from developmental language disorder. NIDCD supports the disorder statements.
- Structural plasticity: contrast Mårtensson et al.'s intensive interpreter training findings with the 160-participant, 11-week randomized older-adult study. MRI changes are not direct neuron counts or proof of improved intelligence.
- Memory: Marian and Neisser's Russian-English autobiographical-memory research concerns retrieval context, not total memory capacity.
- Dementia: Brini et al. (2020) found later diagnosis on average, without a statistically significant reduction in dementia risk. The 3.3-year pooled diagnosis association is not a causal promise or individual forecast.
- Nature Aging (2025): the 86,149-person dataset uses country-level multilingualism exposure. Explicitly avoid describing this as individual bilingual histories, MRI brain age or a dementia-prevention trial. Link the October 5, 2026 author correction concerning the country-label mismatch and Slovak subsample.
- Later-life learning: report mixed randomized intervention findings, including the null Age-Well cognitive analysis and qualified response-inhibition result.
- Sources are linked at the relevant paragraphs in all four editions and listed in article references/schema. PubMed and PMC intermittently returned anti-bot pages during research; publisher pages and university-held papers were used where necessary.
