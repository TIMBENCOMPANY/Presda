# Butterfly effect: editorial verification
Reviewed October 15, 2026.

Original evergreen Science feature, approximately 2,000 English words, with complete French, Spanish and Arabic editions. All 44 content blocks and contextual citations have translation parity.

Key distinctions:
- The 1961 restart used rounded printout values in a twelve-equation simulation. The 1963 convection paper used three equations. These are not conflated.
- Chaos is deterministic sensitive dependence, not synonymous with stochastic randomness, and not a claim that all nonlinear dynamics are chaotic.
- The 1972 butterfly/tornado question is a metaphor, not evidence that a named insect caused a real hurricane. Storm energy is not attributed to the insect.
- No universal numerical weather forecast expiry date is asserted. Weather trajectories and climate statistics are distinguished.
- The 2008 plankton result is restricted to the experimental food web; its 15–30-day horizon is not generalized.
- The original 1990 OGY paper is cited at DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.64.1196, rather than its separate correction.
- Artistic hero and dough analogy are labeled as illustrations, not scientific simulations.
