# Princess Diana feature: editorial and rights record

Publication date: 17 October 2026. Category: Paparazzi, using the existing PRESDA category.

## Scope and evidence

Original evergreen biography in English, French, Arabic and Spanish. The editions preserve the same 41-block narrative and 18 sources. The supplied portrait is explicitly identified as an editorial illustration. Four authentic historical photographs have separate localized captions and license links; source records and originals are recorded in image-rights.json.

The biography distinguishes Diana's documented life, public testimony and interpretation. No reconstructed dialogue, unsupported diagnoses, worldwide viewing totals or conspiracy assertions are included. The 1995 interview is contextualized by the 2021 Dyson findings on deception and BBC failures. Dyson is not represented as an inquiry into the Paris crash. Operation Paget (2006) and the inquest verdict (2008) are described separately. Unlawful killing is explained in relation to gross negligence, not premeditated assassination or criminal convictions at the inquest.

Sources include the Royal Household, National AIDS Trust, WHO, Centrepoint, Historic Royal Palaces, the original Dyson report, Metropolitan Police Operation Paget report, contemporary AP/UPI/Reuters coverage, HALO Trust, UN, Nobel Committee and Westminster Abbey. All source URLs are carried in the article and localized records. Original Dyson PDF was downloaded and its conclusions read directly.

## Image decisions

- Hero: user-supplied editorial portrait; not represented as authentic archival evidence.
- Wedding: Colin Smith, July 1981 procession view, CC BY-SA 2.0. Caption explicitly avoids implying this is a close-up of Diana.
- White House: US federal photograph, 9 November 1985, public domain. Existing archival crop identified.
- Paris: Keimzelle, 1 September 2017 memorial, CC BY-SA 4.0. Explicitly not a 1997 crash photograph.
- Funeral: Jialiang Gao, 6 September 1997, CC BY-SA 3.0. Original small resolution retained without enlargement.

Full composition preserved during WebP conversion, with responsive derivatives and the same individual ShareAlike licenses where applicable. No graphic death photograph or unlicensed news-agency image was substituted.
