Carlos Alcaraz won the 2026 Erste Bank Open in Vienna on 1 November, defeating Alex de Minaur 6–1, 6–4. The tournament’s official report confirms the result, an 87-minute final and Alcaraz’s first title at the event. The second seed finished his championship week without losing a set. ## A fast opening, a more competitive second set

The final combined two different scorelines: a commanding first set and a second in which the margin narrowed. That distinction matters more than calling the whole match a procession. A player who loses the opening set heavily can still force the eventual champion to rebuild pressure rather than simply coast to the finish.

The official account describes de Minaur stabilising his service games and saving a break point to reach 4–4 in the second set. Alcaraz then closed the match on the Australian’s serve. The score records the outcome; those changes in resistance explain why a straight-sets final can still contain distinct phases.

## The route through the semi-finals

Alcaraz reached his first Vienna final by beating Daniil Medvedev 6–4, 7–6(2) on 31 October. That official semi-final result supplies useful context: the champion had already negotiated a tie-break against a former Vienna winner. Winning without dropping a set did not mean every set of his week was comfortable. De Minaur earned the other place in the final by defeating top seed Alexander Zverev 6–4, 1–6, 7–6(4). His progress prevented a final between the top two seeds. The eventual runner-up therefore arrived with a substantial win of his own, rather than merely filling the opposite side of the championship scorecard. ## Why Vienna belongs in its own category

Vienna is an ATP 500 event on hard courts in the official 2026 tour calendar. It is not a Grand Slam. Tournament categories distinguish the competition’s place on the circuit; a Vienna championship should be counted as a tour title, not added to a player’s major total. That simple distinction keeps a successful week in its proper sporting context. [ATP Tour, official 2026 calendar](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/what-is-the-2026-atp-tour-calendar/)

Alcaraz’s presence was itself a late addition: organisers announced his entry on 15 October. His only previous Vienna appearance had been in 2021. Five years later, the return ended with a first title. That gives the trophy a particular place in his season: a tournament added late became a championship completed without a lost set.
