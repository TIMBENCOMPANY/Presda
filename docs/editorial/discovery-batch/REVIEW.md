# Discovery features: editorial and implementation record

Reviewed 22 October 2026. Three independent articles, completed in the order dark sky tourism, undersea cables, handedness. Each has 35 aligned content blocks in English, French, Arabic and Spanish. English contains approximately 1,730 to 1,750 words including linked source labels. Translations preserve all sections, qualifications and numerical claims. ## Scope and evidence

- Dark sky tourism is a selection of seven regions, not an unsupported ranking. It separates research-observatory daytime visits from nighttime observing. ESO confirms free prebooked Saturday Paranal visits; IAC describes daytime visits to Roque de los Muchachos. The NPS Natural Bridges vehicle fee is a dated US$20/seven-day example, not a total holiday budget. DarkSky definitions, NASA observing and photography guidance, official operators and tourism authorities support access, seasons and planning. No clear-weather guarantee or generic international safety ranking is made.
- Undersea cables attributes the greater-than-99-percent international-data estimate to ITU and does not equate it with every internet interaction. The chronology distinguishes 1858, 1866, TAT-1 (1956) and TAT-8 (1988). ITU's February 2026 approximate annual fault and fishing/anchoring estimates are explicitly contextualized. ICPC/UNEP, NEC, ASN, TeleGeography and official project announcements support construction, repair, routes and ownership. Design capacity, equipped capacity and actual traffic are distinguished. No particular cable failure is attributed to sabotage without evidence.
- Handedness uses the 2020 prevalence meta-analysis (about 10.6 percent), MedlinePlus, the 2020 genetic association study, language-dominance research, the 2018 IQ review and 2025 creativity review. Genetics is probabilistic; a hand is not an intelligence test; hemisphere specialization is not a personality binary. Sports findings and evolutionary hypotheses are separated. Documented forced switching is not generalized to all cultures or used to invent medical consequences. Leonardo and McCartney examples have institutional or first-party sources.
- Each English source link is attached to the relevant paragraph. The generator reproduces that link at the same paragraph in every translation, with localized labels. Source lists remain available through the existing Article schema. Existing relevant PRESDA pages are linked, with locale-aware routing supplied by the existing renderer.
