## Evidence and editorial decisions

- Denisovans: Rao and colleagues' Nature paper, published 9 September 2026, molecularly assigns two teeth, two parietal fragments and a partial radius from Bianfu Cave to Denisovans. Five remains do not establish five people. The Chinese Academy of Sciences describes the radius's mosaic anatomy. Xing and colleagues' Nature paper, published 21 October 2026, reports proteins from two Xujiayao specimens and broader morphological comparisons. Molecular affiliation is distinguished from a further fossil's proposed affiliation through anatomical similarity. The ERC's 2025 Penghu account explains the protein method. No complete genome, individual face or pigmentation is inferred from these proteins.
- Electronic skin: Cambridge and UCL's June 2025 accounts and Hardman, Thuruthel and Iida's Science Robotics paper document conductive hydrogel, a hand-shaped demonstrator, 32 wrist electrodes, electrical impedance tomography and machine learning. The electrode count is not a receptor count. Bench demonstrations are distinguished from real-world grasping, durability and subjective sensation. Stanford's May 2023 account documents soft electronics producing nerve-like impulses; it does not establish restored human sensation in a patient. The grasping example is explicitly illustrative.
- Glass octopus: Schmidt Ocean Institute's 2021 Phoenix Islands expedition release and field log document two observations, the first at 680 metres, and the conspicuous eyes, optic nerve and digestive tract of the transparent animal. These are observations of a known species, not a 2026 species discovery or a population estimate. Transparency is not literal invisibility. No unsupported adult size, diet, life span or bioluminescence is invented.
- Lev Yashin: UEFA's winners list, updated 26 October 2026, confirms the 1963 award and unique goalkeeper status. UEFA's historical profiles and EURO 1960 team support the dark kit, nicknames, agility and European title. Olympedia supplies Dynamo Moscow, five national leagues, three Soviet Cups and the 1956 Olympic gold. UEFA explains the separate Yashin Trophy introduced in 2019. Unverified penalty totals, invented quotations and claims that he invented every modern goalkeeping technique are excluded.
- Eternal Flame Falls: Erie County's official Chestnut Ridge Park page establishes the New York setting. Etiope, Drobniak and Schimmelmann's 2013 Marine and Petroleum Geology paper documents the predominantly thermogenic seep and approximately 35% combined ethane and propane by volume. Formation of gas, migration and surface combustion are separate processes. The ACS Chemical & Engineering News 2019 feature documents reignition; the name does not establish uninterrupted burning. No invented first ignition, volcanic explanation or proof of a new room-temperature fuel-producing mechanism is claimed.
