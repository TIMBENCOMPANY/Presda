# Extinct Animals and Tartaria publication batch

Prepared 20 October 2026. Two independent articles, four complete editions each.

## Editorial scope

Extinct Animals follows the user's approved order exactly: Smilodon, Megalodon, Titanoboa, Megatherium, Deinosuchus, Quetzalcoatlus, Thylacine, Dodo, Great Auk, Passenger Pigeon, Steller's Sea Cow, Quagga, Irish Elk, Arthropleura, Dunkleosteus, Helicoprion, Woolly Rhinoceros, Haast's Eagle, Moa, Pyrenean Ibex.

The article distinguishes genera, species and subspecies, fossil occurrence dates and actual extinction dates, documented deaths and last wild records, and established causes versus hypotheses. The English source has approximately 3,300 words including citations. All translations preserve the same 67 blocks and citation targets.

Tartaria distinguishes the real historical geographic label and real Tatar peoples, Mongol states and Turkic khanates from unsupported modern global-empire, Mud Flood and free-energy claims. The English source has approximately 1,850 words including citations. All translations preserve its 38 blocks and citation targets.

Sources are linked at the relevant passages and collected in each article's reference list. Important checks include the 2019 megalodon extinction reassessment, 2023 Dunkleosteus body-proportion revision, 2024 Arthropleura head study, museum collections, New Zealand Birds Online, the Library of Congress map history and original architectural catalog, UNESCO and the Encyclopedia of Chicago.

## Images and rights

The supplied original JPEG heroes are copied byte for byte. Both receive localized AI-illustration captions. The extinct-animal museum is imaginary and not a depiction of coexistence. The Tartaria image is a fictional symbolic composite, not historical evidence.

`media-rights.json` records the 23 selected supporting images, source pages, original URLs, creators, licenses, permission evidence, transformations and verification date. `image-candidates.json` preserves candidate metadata, including rejected mismatches. LOC catalog records are saved separately. Twenty correctly matched images appear directly after the twenty animal headings. Tartaria includes two historical maps and an 1893 architectural plan.

Only public-domain/CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA material was selected. No NC or ND license is used. CC BY-SA derivatives retain their original license through the visible license link. Source attribution requirements are retained, including BrokenSphere's requested attribution. Original compositions are preserved in WebP conversions. Responsive variants are not upscaled. Small archival photographs are capped at their intrinsic width. Scientific casts, mounted skeletons, preserved specimens and historical plates are identified rather than represented as photographs of living prehistoric animals.

The contact sheet was visually reviewed for identity against the source descriptions. The two approved hero checksums are asserted in the validation script.
