# Félicette editorial review

Reviewed 16 October 2026. Article 273, Science. Slug: felicette-french-cat-space-tragic-end.

## Evidence and editorial choices

The four editions contain 33 matching blocks and 12 unique cited sources. English is approximately 1,600 words including headings and citation labels. No invented quotations, private thoughts, final scenes or researcher remorse. All sources are linked beside the relevant discussion and in the bibliography.

- NASA's animal-spaceflight chronology confirms 18 October 1963, Véronique AGI 47, parachute recovery and the unsuccessful second feline mission on 24 October. The article distinguishes suborbital flight from orbit and avoids calling Félicette the only cat ever launched.
- CNES's history supports its creation on 19 December 1961 and the 1965 Diamant milestone. ESA's HSR-37 by Hervé Moulin supports the French sounding-rocket context and Hector's 22 February 1961 experiment.
- Air & Cosmos's historical account supports CERMA, implanted neurological recording, conditioning and the limited measured result. Claims about emotions, general safety or the absence of suffering are not inferred from physiological signals.
- Smithsonian's 28 January 2020 report supports post-flight euthanasia for brain examination. Exact death date is not asserted. The text distinguishes research killing from relief of a documented terminal illness.
- Sources differ over altitude, elapsed time and early biography. Use approximately 150 km and a brief suborbital flight, without combining incompatible precision. Describe the Paris-stray account as commonly reported, not a fully established biography.
- ESA's Gagarin chronology prevents the false claim that Félicette enabled the first human spaceflight. ESA's microgravity explanation supports free fall rather than disappearance of gravity.
- NC3Rs supports the 1959 origins and modern meaning of the 3Rs. Do not retroactively claim that this mission followed modern safeguards.
- Space.com corroborates the 2017 campaign and December 2019 memorial. No unverified exact unveiling day, fundraising total or invented quote.
- CNES audiovisual record cnes-0089-02-VF-S005 is linked as an archival resource. Its description, not the entire film, was inspected. Former CNES Observatoire and Spacegate pages could not be retrieved and are not used as read sources.
