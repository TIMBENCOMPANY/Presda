Two people arrive in an unfamiliar town hoping to become parents. Around that intimate ambition, Martin Scorsese is building his next film with Leonardo DiCaprio and Jennifer Lawrence. What Happens at Night is a confirmed production, not merely a proposed reunion: Apple announced principal photography in the Czech Republic on March 19, 2026. The actors also serve as producers. [Apple announcement via the Czech Film Commission](https://filmcommission.gov.cz/en/apple-original-films-what-happens-at-night-from-martin-scorsese-starring-leonardo-dicaprio-and-jennifer-lawrence-begins-principal-photography/)

The appeal is easy to understand before a frame of the finished film has been seen. Two stars associated with very different kinds of screen intensity are entering a story built around disorientation. But the distinction between anticipation and evidence matters: an impressive creative team is a reason for interest, not a substitute for watching the film.

## What is Scorsese's new film about?

The official premise follows a married American couple travelling to a small, snow-covered European town to adopt a baby. Apple Studios is making the feature with STUDIOCANAL, adapting Peter Cameron's novel of the same name. Beyond that outline, detailed predictions about the ending or individual characters would go beyond the announcement. [Confirmed production and premise](https://filmcommission.gov.cz/en/apple-original-films-what-happens-at-night-from-martin-scorsese-starring-leonardo-dicaprio-and-jennifer-lawrence-begins-principal-photography/)

### A novel that makes uncertainty its setting

Catapult published Cameron's novel in August 2020. Its description places the couple in an ageing grand hotel, surrounded by unsettling guests. That literary starting point offers a useful way into the project: a hotel promises shelter and order, yet here it becomes a place in which neither seems dependable. [The publisher's book record](https://books.catapult.co/books/what-happens-at-night/)

The adaptation's central challenge, as an editorial reading of that premise, is how to make uncertainty visible. Prose can keep a reader inside an unresolved thought; film gives a face, a room and a sound to what is happening. The choice of what to show, and what to leave outside the frame, could therefore matter as much as the unfolding mystery. This is a question about adaptation, not a claim about scenes in the finished production.

## DiCaprio and Lawrence already have a shared screen history

The pairing reunites the leads of Adam McKay's Don't Look Up. Netflix's 2021 film cast them as astronomers trying to persuade a distracted world to take an approaching comet seriously. Its premise placed their characters in a public struggle to make other people acknowledge a danger. [Netflix's original cast interview and announcement](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/dont-look-up-trailer-jennifer-lawrence-leonardo-dicaprio-adam-mckay)

The announced Scorsese story shifts the scale from planetary emergency to a couple's private journey. That contrast helps explain the reunion's interest without assuming that it will repeat the earlier film's comic rhythm. Familiar actors can carry a viewer into an unfamiliar tone, but the tone itself will depend on direction, performance and editing.

### A continuing relationship with Scorsese and Apple

The production follows Scorsese and DiCaprio's work with Apple on Killers of the Flower Moon. Scorsese directs and produces through Sikelia Productions; Daniel Lupi joins DiCaprio and Lawrence as a producer. The official cast also names Patricia Clarkson, Mads Mikkelsen, Jared Harris and Welker White. [Production credits and cast](https://filmcommission.gov.cz/en/apple-original-films-what-happens-at-night-from-martin-scorsese-starring-leonardo-dicaprio-and-jennifer-lawrence-begins-principal-photography/)

Producer credits are part of the story, although they do not disclose who controls every creative or commercial decision. A credit alone cannot establish salary, ownership, final-cut rights or the division of day-to-day work. The broader shift towards stars developing projects is explored in PRESDA's profile of [Margot Robbie as a producer](https://presda.com/articles/margot-robbie-hollywood-star-powerful-producer/).

## What the production reporting adds

The Film Stage's March 19 account identifies Patrick Marber as the screenwriter adapting Cameron's book, with Rodrigo Prieto on cinematography and Thelma Schoonmaker editing. These craft appointments help explain attention to the project, but the article's expectation of a 2027 arrival was the writer's assessment, not a release date announced by the studio. [The Film Stage's production report](https://thefilmstage.com/first-look-at-martin-scorseses-what-happens-at-night-starring-leonardo-dicaprio-and-jennifer-lawrence/)

### The reported Charli xcx appearance

On October 23, Variety reported, citing sources, that Charli xcx has a cameo. It said representatives for the singer, Apple and STUDIOCANAL had not immediately responded to its request for comment. That makes the appearance a trade-publication report, distinct from the performers named in the March official announcement. Her role was not disclosed. [Variety's October casting report](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/global/charli-xcx-martin-scorsese-what-happens-at-night-41796/)

Keeping those categories separate allows readers to follow a developing film without turning every addition to the conversation into studio confirmation. It also avoids assigning a character or storyline to a performer on the basis of speculation.

## Is there a confirmed release date?

No release date was identified in the announcements and current reporting reviewed for this article on October 29, 2026. Variety's October 23 report likewise said a date had not been set. A theatrical opening, streaming debut, festival premiere, runtime and audience reception should not be inferred from a production announcement. [The latest release-status reporting reviewed](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/global/charli-xcx-martin-scorsese-what-happens-at-night-41796/)

For now, the strongest story is the project itself: a literary mystery, a substantial ensemble and a reunion that moves DiCaprio and Lawrence into a different dramatic space. The finished film will eventually answer questions that casting announcements cannot. Until then, the most useful preview explains the confirmed foundation while leaving room for Scorsese's version to reveal itself.
