## Historical decisions

- Bengal's 1414 presentation is established; Africa is the animal's natural origin. East African connections and Malindi are qualified. No exact capture site or shipping itinerary invented.
- Church (2004) differentiates the Bengal mission's 1414 return from Zheng He's fourth expedition returning in 1415. No personal escort claim.
- Yongle accession in 1402 distinguished from reign-era dates beginning 1403; death 1424. Met exhibition overview has inconsistent ending year, so chronological dates cross-checked with Library of Congress and Dreyer catalogue.
- Laufer (Field Museum, 1928) is public-domain historical scholarship, read critically, not used for outdated voyage chronology or racial language. Documents subsequent 1415 Malindi gift and limits of animal-care records.
- Qilin interpretation does not imply all observers were credulous or that Yongle's private belief is known.
- Smithsonian institution sources distinguish a bibliographic catalogue from direct access to the book. Smithsonian Ming visual-culture material supports the court-art context.
- No copied quotations, invented dialogue, unsupported dimensions of treasure ships, or definitive lifespan for the 1414 giraffe.
