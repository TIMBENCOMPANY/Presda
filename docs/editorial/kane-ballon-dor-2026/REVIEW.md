## Source issues resolved

Search results included prematurely released winner profiles for several nominees. They were excluded as result evidence. Indexed award landing pages still showed pre-ceremony information, but the Bundesliga's live page subsequently confirmed the final result. Some sources gave inconsistent aggregate appearance counts; the article uses the club's 51 and England's 16 separately and does not publish an aggregate appearance number. Club goals and combined club/country goals are distinguished.
