The orange-red form looks like a car, but the point is not to drive it. On November 2, 2026, Kia announced a full-scale EV4 GT clay tribute presented to Rafael Nadal at his academy in Manacor, Mallorca. It connects the material of automotive model-making with the surface on which Nadal built his greatest sporting reputation. The result is a commemorative sculpture, not a road-ready electric vehicle. [Kia: November 2, 2026 EV4 GT clay tribute announcement](https://worldwide.kia.com/en/newsroom/view?id=161961)

## What exactly did Kia make?

Kia’s campaign describes a one-off sculpture inspired by the proportions of the EV4 GT. Designers and model-makers shaped its surfaces at full scale, using automotive modelling material combined with clay from courts associated with Nadal’s career. White design tape picks out contours that also recall court markings. The resemblance to a finished vehicle is part of the visual joke: something normally expected to move has become a monument. [Kia: King of Clay sculpture and making-of](https://www.kia.com/eu/about-kia/experience-kia/brand/sponsoring/king-of-clay/)

### A sculpture is not a new vehicle specification

The distinction matters when the image travels without its explanation. This tribute does not establish a clay-bodied production car, a new powertrain, a range figure or a vehicle available to buy. The EV4 GT name identifies the design reference. Treating the object as a functioning prototype would turn a carefully staged brand story into an inaccurate technology report.

## Why 63 clay-court titles matter

The number refers to men’s tour-level singles titles on clay, not 63 Grand Slams. ATP’s career account credits Nadal with an Open Era men’s record of 63 clay trophies and 22 major singles championships overall. Fourteen of those majors came at Roland-Garros. Keeping the categories separate explains the scale of the tribute without inflating it: a surface record and a Grand Slam total measure different achievements. [ATP Tour: Rafael Nadal’s career and retirement](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/rafael-nadal-retirement-press-release)

Guinness records the 63rd title at the 2022 French Open. Its breakdown shows how concentrated that success was: 14 titles in Paris, 12 in Barcelona, 11 in Monte Carlo and 10 in Rome. Those four tournaments account for 47 of the 63. The sculpture’s material therefore works as a sporting reference rather than a random decorative finish. It evokes repeated success in places where clay was the setting, not just the colour of the court. [Guinness World Records: 63 men’s clay-court singles titles](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/497946-most-clay-court-singles-titles-open-era)

## A partnership that began before the first major

Kia’s history of the relationship begins in 2004 with support from Kia Spain. Nadal won his first Roland-Garros title in 2005 and became the company’s global brand ambassador in 2006. Kia renewed the partnership in October 2025, after his competitive retirement. These dates describe stages of the same relationship, not contradictory starting points: local sponsorship came first, followed by the global appointment. [Kia: partnership renewal in October 2025](https://www.kia.com/th/en/util/news/kia-partnership-rafael-nadal-2025.html)

## Why the business story extends beyond a gift

As a piece of sponsorship, the sculpture makes an existing association visible in one image. Clay has a credible role in both tennis and vehicle design, so the link requires less explanation than a generic celebrity appearance. The academy setting adds context. That is an interpretation of the campaign’s construction, not evidence that it increased car sales or changed consumer behaviour. No campaign return on investment can be calculated from an unveiling alone.

## What the November announcement establishes

The official announcement is dated November 2, 2026 and identifies the academy in Mallorca as the presentation venue. It describes a tribute that combines automotive modelling clay and material from courts connected with Nadal’s titles. Those are the documented claims. The public account is not a published independent audit of the material’s provenance, and the announcement does not turn the sculpture into a road-legal product. [Kia: November 2, 2026 EV4 GT clay tribute announcement](https://worldwide.kia.com/en/newsroom/view?id=161961)

## A sporting legacy, made tangible

The most interesting part of the tribute is its restraint. It does not need to break a speed record or promise a revolutionary material. It asks a familiar object to carry the memory of an unfamiliar level of sporting achievement. Read as sculpture, it brings Nadal’s clay-court story and Kia’s design process into the same frame. Read as a new car, it tells the wrong story.
