## Evidence and editorial decisions

- Volvo: Reuters' October 23 update on Euronext supports an almost 30% intraday fall and 2,500 additional positions. No closing price is asserted. The official investor page reports SEK 66.9bn revenue, SEK -5.6bn EBIT and -8.4% margin. The SEK -3.7bn adjusted result is explicitly distinguished. Implementation of staffing measures and full financial savings have different dates. Volvo Cars is distinguished from Volvo Group. The invented numeric display in the approved image is expressly disclaimed.
- John Legend: official artist website, Universal Music Canada announcement and Jonathan Landrum Jr.'s AP interview establish the date, 16 tracks, producer, collaborations and creative process. Three introductory tracks are distinguished from complete songs. Descriptions of influences are attributed to the creators. No invented listening review, chart position, sales total or critical consensus. The AI hero is not described as an official cover. AP and the September release disagree about the month for Imitation of Life, so no theatrical opening date is asserted.
- Ebola: WHO's October 20 Nigeria statement establishes one imported case and an initial 44 contacts. WHO report 23 has Congo data through October 19; ECDC's October 22 update has Congo data through October 20. Both cutoffs are printed. Africa CDC's contact/death indicators and airport investigation are attributed through AP's October 22 briefing coverage, not invented as a directly inspected Africa CDC dashboard. The exposure location is unresolved. Kenya's linked cluster, Nigeria's importation, Uganda's earlier ended outbreak, France's isolated imported case and Germany's medical evacuations are distinguished. Germany evacuations are not double-counted. Research protocols and vaccine candidates are not presented as proven protection. WHO guidance supports supportive care and transmission explanations; the report gives no diagnosis of an individual reader.
- Tunisia: Tarek Amara's Reuters reporting on October 22 is the principal event source; October 17 and August Reuters reports and August/September AP coverage supply dated background. Family claims, local media accounts and the lack of immediate ministry comment remain separate. No combined protest death toll, unverified arrest tally or cause-of-death determination is invented. The Sfax student's death is not presented as a death in a police clash. Saied's general position is attributed and is not misrepresented as a specific response to the latest disputed deaths.

All source links are retained alongside the relevant paragraphs and in the existing source-list/schema pipeline. No direct quotations are introduced. Earlier reporting is dated and is not passed off as a new interview. No em dashes or editorial double hyphens are used. Manuscripts are concise news features rather than padding to an arbitrary evergreen word target.
