Cristiano Ronaldo's 2008 season has won the Ballon d'Or of All Time public vote, returning attention to the Manchester United campaign that brought him his first Ballon d'Or. The result celebrates an enduring sporting memory. It does not add a sixth official Ballon d'Or to his record, and it does not establish a new trophy awarded by the annual prize's journalist jury. [Official B.O.A.T. results](https://ballondor.com/vote)

## What Ronaldo actually won

The B.O.A.T. exercise asked supporters to choose among award-winning seasons associated with ten players who had won the Ballon d'Or at least twice. Voting ran from October 1 to October 27, 2026, with the result announced on October 28. The official results place Ronaldo's 2008 season first and Lionel Messi's 2012 season second. [Ballon d'Or: how B.O.A.T. worked](https://ballondor.com/fr/news/posts/qui-sera-le-boat-le-ballon-dor-of-all-time)

The unit of comparison was a season, not an entire career evaluated through a common statistical formula. That distinction helps explain why different years belonging to the same footballer could compete. A supporter might favour the breakthrough of a young winger, another a mature goalscorer, and another a season defined by a particularly memorable trophy.

### A paid vote with repeat participation

The published conditions required the purchase of a €1 digital photograph for an entry. They also allowed multiple entries, with a separate purchase for each. This makes the outcome a paid participation vote rather than a one-person, one-vote survey of football supporters. Readers should know that mechanism before treating the result as a measure of universal agreement. [B.O.A.T. participation conditions](https://ballondor.com/voting-terms-and-conditions)

The results page says 5,009 people voted more than once. That disclosure is relevant to interpretation, but it does not by itself tell us how repeat entries were distributed between candidates. Nor does it establish wrongdoing. The rules permitted repeat participation; the editorial responsibility is to explain it, not to infer manipulation without evidence.

## Why the 2008 season still resonates

Ronaldo's Manchester United campaign combined individual production with the Premier League and Champions League titles. UEFA's contemporary report records 42 goals in all competitions, including 31 in the league. He was 23 when he received the 2008 Ballon d'Or, the first of the five official awards that would shape the public image of his career. [UEFA's contemporary 2008 account](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01d4-0e707fe4919f-772c18bc5bc3-1000--ballon-d-or-crowns-ronaldo-s-golden-year/)

Those achievements provide a concrete basis for understanding the season's appeal. It was a campaign in which scoring, team success and a conspicuous rise in individual status coincided. The fan vote cannot tell us which of those qualities each participant valued. It can show that this particular version of Ronaldo remains powerful in football's collective memory.

### Manchester, Madrid and five official awards

His later Ballons d'Or came in 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017 as a Real Madrid player. Madrid's response to the B.O.A.T. result highlights that sequence, linking the Manchester breakthrough to the years that followed in Spain. Those five annual awards remain a separate record from the 2026 public exercise. [Real Madrid's official response](https://www.realmadrid.com/en-US/news/club/latest-news/cristiano-ronaldo-elegido-mejor-balon-de-oro-de-la-historia-28-10-2026)

Manchester United's report says Ronaldo's 2017 season finished third, behind his 2008 campaign and Messi's 2012 season. It also notes that the organizers are not presenting a physical award or trophy for this poll. ## How this differs from the annual Ballon d'Or

The men's annual prize uses a journalist jury representing the top 100 countries in the relevant FIFA ranking. Its published criteria prioritize individual performance, followed by team achievements and then class and fair play. The 2026 rules describe a ranked selection from a shortlist, rather than purchases by supporters. [Official Ballon d'Or 2026 rules](https://ballondor.com/fr/news/posts/tous-les-criteres-et-le-reglement-complet-du-ballon-dor-2026)

Neither process should be described as the other. A jury award and a public engagement campaign ask different questions and recruit different participants. Winning the second does not retroactively change the first. Equally, explaining those limits does not require dismissing the pleasure supporters take in revisiting a favourite player or season.

### Is B.O.A.T. a new Ballon d'Or or Super Ballon d'Or?

No. This result belongs to the named public vote. It should not be counted as another annual Ballon d'Or, described as a new Super Ballon d'Or, or illustrated as an authenticated trophy ceremony. Keeping the labels precise prevents a popular online result from becoming an inaccurate entry in a player's official honours list.

## What a fan vote can, and cannot, prove

Paid participation, self-selection and repeat entries mean the outcome is not a representative opinion poll. There is no basis here for a statistical claim that a specified proportion of all football fans regard Ronaldo as the greatest player ever. The official result supports the narrower statement that his 2008 season topped this particular exercise under its published rules.

The distinction is especially important when headlines travel without their explanations. A short phrase such as “all-time winner” can imply a definitive historical verdict. A fuller account gives the result its proper scale: an expression of fan engagement with an exceptional season, alongside the separate history of football's annual awards. PRESDA's [PSG transformation feature](https://presda.com/articles/nasser-al-khelaifi-psg-qsi-70-million-transformation/) explores another example of how sporting achievement and a global football brand interact.

Ronaldo's supporters have a clear result to celebrate. Readers have an equally clear reason to keep the category attached to it. The 2008 campaign won B.O.A.T.; the five official Ballons d'Or remain five.
