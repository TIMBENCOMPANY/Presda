Boca Juniors won Argentina’s 2026 annual league title on November 7 after defeating Defensa y Justicia 1–0 at La Bombonera. Substitute Enner Valencia struck deep into second-half stoppage time, turning an anxious final round into a celebration. Boca’s own report confirms the Tabla Anual crown and the eruption of relief around the stadium. This was the championship awarded for the annual standings, not the separate Clausura title. [Boca Juniors: official title and match report](https://www.bocajuniors.com.ar/noticias/campeones)

## The goal that changed the final table

The decisive moment came with Rosario Central already putting Boca under pressure through its own victory. Valencia capitalised on a loose ball after goalkeeper Matías Borgogno failed to deal with the delivery into the area. His finish secured the only goal of the match. Reports describe the action at the very end of added time; the reliable point across the accounts is its timing after the regulation 90 minutes, with almost no opportunity left for another twist. [TyC Sports: the final round and stoppage-time winner](https://www.tycsports.com/boca-juniors/que-necesitan-los-tres-equipos-con-chances-de-ganar-la-tabla-anual-boca-argentinos-juniors-rosario-central-id770760.html)

Boca finished with 63 points, one ahead of Rosario Central’s 62. Argentinos Juniors ended on 60 after losing 2–0 to Huracán, while Central defeated Estudiantes de Río Cuarto 2–0. Those simultaneous results explain why a single late goal carried such weight. It did more than improve Boca’s goal difference: it supplied the win and three points needed to finish above the nearest challenger. ## What exactly did Boca win?

Article 24 of the LPF’s 2026 Primera División regulations combines points from the opening zone phases of the Apertura and Clausura into the Tabla General de Posiciones. The team finishing first is declared Campeón de Liga 2026, a national title. Knockout advancement is a different measure. Winning the annual table rewards points accumulated across those regular phases rather than winning the Clausura’s final match. [LPF: 2026 Primera División regulations, article 24](https://www.ligaprofesional.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Reglamento-Torneos-LPF-Primera-2026-1.pdf)

### Why the Clausura distinction matters

The Argentine Football Association recognises Boca as the 2026 league champion on the basis of the annual table. Calling the team annual league champions is therefore justified. Calling this result a Clausura championship would attach the celebration to a different competition outcome. The distinction is especially important for international readers accustomed to a domestic season with a single league trophy and one final standings table. ## A tense match becomes a stadium-wide celebration

Boca’s official account describes the crowd’s pent-up emotion breaking loose when Valencia scored, followed by the opportunity to celebrate the crown with its own supporters. The setting was part of the story: a title could be secured in front of the home crowd, but only if the team found the result. Rodolfo Arruabarrena’s side reached that moment after consecutive wins over Talleres, Independiente and River Plate, before adding Defensa y Justicia to the sequence. ## What the annual crown does and does not settle

EFE reports that the title also secures Boca a place in the 2027 Copa Libertadores group stage. Other competitions remain separate opportunities, including the Clausura knockout route. Their outcomes cannot be read into the November 7 celebration. A trophy already won and a trophy still being pursued are different facts, even when both appear in the same club’s end-of-season ambitions. [EFE: Valencia winner and final annual standings](https://efe.com/deportes/2026-11-08/boca-juniors-campeon-tabla-anual-enner-valencia/)

## The result in perspective

The annual table explains consistency; the final goal explains how close the finish became. Both belong in the account. Reducing the story to one spectacular instant misses the points earned earlier, while treating it only as a standings calculation misses the uncertainty of the last match. Boca’s achievement combines those two scales: a season’s accumulation decided in its final, nerve-racking moments.
