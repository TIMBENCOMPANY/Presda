### What does 7-6(5) mean?

The set finished seven games to six, with Alcaraz winning the tie-break seven points to five. The number in parentheses records the losing player’s tie-break points. It is not an extra set or a seven-games-to-five score. Explaining the notation matters because the first set’s closeness is central to the match: two points separated the players at the decisive stage.

At 6-5, he broke again to finish the match. Europa Press gives a duration of one hour and 59 minutes. The score was straight sets, but the route through it required repeated responses to trouble. ## A different ending from the US Open

Their previous Grand Slam meeting had ended the other way. Shelton defeated Alcaraz in a five-set US Open quarter-final in September, as the tournament’s own report records. Paris therefore reversed a recent result without erasing it. It was a fresh final on an indoor hard court, played under a best-of-three-sets format, rather than a continuation of the same match or the same competitive conditions. ## What the players said afterwards

Alcaraz praised Shelton’s level and resilience, while describing the Paris title as a goal his team had pursued through earlier disappointments. Shelton congratulated him and highlighted the difficulty of winning consecutive indoor titles in Vienna and Paris. These reactions were reported from the ceremony by Sky Sports. They point to mutual respect after a close final, rather than a rivalry that needs invented hostility to make it compelling.
