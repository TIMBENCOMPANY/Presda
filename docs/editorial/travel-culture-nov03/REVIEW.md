# Editorial and hero review: travel and culture batch, 3 November 2026

Five evergreen Article records and fifteen complete translations.

## Editorial decisions

- Istanbul cats: neighbourhood relationships and professional care; no unsupported citywide population count or single-origin legend. Kedi is dated 2016, with municipal care information distinguished from a district programme.
- Strasbourg: a documented historical disturbance; Frau Troffea is tied to later narration. No verified death toll or settled retrospective diagnosis is claimed. Poisoning and socially transmitted trance are hypotheses.
- Blood Falls: intermittent iron-rich brine at Taylor Glacier; nanosphere work is dated 2023. The 2026 marine-community findings support an origin hypothesis without dating isolation exactly. The supplied waterfall is not a calibrated field observation.
- Pamukkale: carbonate travertine, not snow or table salt. USGS is used for comparative carbonate chemistry, not to imply identical volcanic systems. UNESCO’s mixed site includes Hierapolis; pool conditions and access are not guaranteed.
- Ibn Battuta: Africa, Asia and Europe include al-Andalus. The Rihla is a retrospectively edited narrative combining observation and other material, not a GPS-verified diary. No exact distance record or authenticated portrait is asserted.

## Artwork and design

The five approved JPEGs were copied byte for byte to descriptive article-specific filenames. Source and published SHA-256 values match. No source resizing, cropping, metadata injection, colour edit or image replacement was performed. Existing responsive WebP and JPEG social derivatives are prepared by the normal build pipeline. The attachments are 1280 pixels wide; they are not artificially enlarged to claim 1672 native pixels.

The new records reuse the existing bounded Bruno hero contract. Desktop uses Bruno’s viewport cap with a native-aspect ceiling to avoid side cropping. Vertical cropping follows the previously approved Bruno framing. Mobile uses the same natural image and copy layout as Bruno. No CSS override is added. Red, white and yellow highlights, category, publication date, subtitle and reading time use existing components.

Images are credited as editorial images supplied for PRESDA. Original creator and reuse licence were not supplied, so neither an invented photographer nor licence is attached. Historical reconstructions and conceptual scientific depictions are disclosed in all languages.

## Publishing checks

Canonical and reciprocal EN/FR/AR/ES plus x-default links use each article’s own route. Article/ImageObject and image sitemap entries use that route, not the homepage. Share metadata uses each hero’s dedicated social JPEG. These are evergreen articles and do not enter the news sitemap.

Research links and the four-language prose are retained in specs.json and individual Markdown files. Browser verification evidence is retained in task outputs.
