## Editorial choices
The requested headline is retained, but the introduction and conclusion explicitly reject illness as a condition of artistic greatness. The biography distinguishes documented episodes from retrospective diagnosis, and formal interpretation from clinical evidence. No quotations or imagined private conversations are invented. The ear incident is described without graphic detail. The death follows the Van Gogh Museum's suicide account without presenting alternative speculation as established history or reducing causation to one factor.

The article corrects the final-painting myth around Wheatfield with Crows, avoids the sweeping claim that only one transaction occurred in Vincent's lifetime, and credits Theo and Jo van Gogh-Bonger. Letter 705 provides the artist's stated intention of repose for The Bedroom; letter 898 describes both sadness and the strengthening countryside. The Starry Night is distinguished from a literal window view or a diagnostic image. The discussion of Irises includes Getty's research on faded red pigment.
