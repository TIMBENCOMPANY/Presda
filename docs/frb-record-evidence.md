## Core verified findings

- Designation FRB 20240304B. MeerTRAP/MeerKAT detection March 4, 2024. New publication October 8, 2026. Preprint August 3, 2025. Do not describe it as newly detected this week.
- Host spectroscopic redshift 2.148; preprint uncertainty ±0.001. NIRCam identifies host, NIRSpec measures spectrum. MeerKAT detects radio pulse, not Webb.
- More than ten billion years of lookback time; emission when Universe about three billion years old. Current age about 13.8 billion years per NASA cosmology explanation. Rounded values, not exact ages.
- Present-day proper separation and luminosity distance are different cosmology-dependent measures. Avoid presenting ten billion light-years as present-day separation. No numerical present-day distance invented.
- Record wording scoped to localized FRB with measured host redshift. Older FRB 20220610A z=1.016±0.002 from Ryder author manuscript https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.04680; about eight billion years from ESO https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2317/. Redshift more than doubles, lookback time does not.
- Young star-forming dwarf host favors prompt magnetar origin, not direct identification. Current ESA statement estimates roughly 30 million years formation timescale. Do not reproduce older preprint's 51.7-million-year value as final result. No precise mass/fluence/energy figure carried from preliminary work without final corroboration.
- ESO's decades-of-Sun-energy analogy concerns older FRB 20220610A only. Do not attach to new burst or confuse radio energy, total solar energy and peak power.
- CHIME Galactic magnetar research https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2863-y supplies established connection, not proof of this source.
- Dispersion traces integrated electrons, not a standalone exact distance. Macquart 2020 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32461651/ ordinary baryon census, not dark matter. Host, diffuse medium, Virgo and foreground structure contributions differentiated.
- Unknowns: emitting object, emission mechanism, repeat activity, sample demographics and unique magnetic reconstruction. No extraterrestrial communication inference.
