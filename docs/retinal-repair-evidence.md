# PRESDA retinal repair feature: evidence record

Verified October 8, 2026. Feature scope is a recent, verified original study, not a claim to have identified every latest ophthalmology paper worldwide.

Main source: Elia, Pauzuolyte, Georgiou and colleagues, Nature Communications 17, 10365, published September 30, 2026. DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78065-z. Original paper, methods, Figure 5A/results, affiliations, limitations and peer review statement inspected. Open access under CC BY 4.0; attribution, license link and adaptation disclosure included.

Study models: patient-derived iPSC retinal pigment epithelium and retinal organoids. RPE donor lines: three affected donors; organoid disease line: one affected donor. Donors are not clinical trial participants receiving treatment. No new animal treatment results or human treatment outcomes in this study.

Graphic values: GFP.AAV control 19.2%; PRPF31.AAV 59.3%. Measurement is internalized photoreceptor outer-segment material as percentage of image area after four hours. Three images per condition for area assay, not a patient sample size. Values copied from Figure 5A results text, not converted into percent vision restored. Zero-baseline bars use a 0 to 100% scale. Reported point values only; no fabricated uncertainty intervals, clinical effect size or success rates. Equivalent server-rendered table, localized labels, limitations and source remain visible without JavaScript.

Main limitations: surviving cells needed; mature culture is not proof of late-stage patient efficacy; dead photoreceptors not replaced; future in vivo dose, timing and long-term safety work explicitly requested. No supported clinical availability year.

Contextual sources: National Eye Institute retinitis pigmentosa and ocular gene therapy core; FDA Luxturna indication and prescribing information; FDA retinal gene therapy development guidance. Luxturna risks explicitly belong to that approved product and procedure, not observed RP11-vector adverse events. Its RPE65 indication is distinct from PRPF31.

Separate regeneration context: Lee and colleagues, Nature Communications, March 25, 2025, DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-58290-8. Prox1 findings in mice and human tissue observations are separately identified, never presented as a successful human treatment or combined with the September study as one trial.
