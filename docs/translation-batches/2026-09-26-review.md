# Review of the first ten multilingual articles

The first batch in `2026-09-26.json` was reread in French, Arabic and Spanish against the full English sources on 26 September 2026. This was a Codex editorial and source review, not independent human certification. The review preserved the original reporting dates, English records, pilot translations and shared article components.

## Corrections

- French: twelve targeted corrections across the trucks, Guinness and Bermuda articles. These improve idiom, restore EV supply-chain specificity and driver/image context, avoid restricting official adjudication to an on-site visit, and identify the Bermuda illustration as fictional rather than documentary evidence.
- Arabic: thirteen wording and terminology corrections across trucks, Beckham, the dolphin experiment, AI companionship, Samsung and Bermuda. They clarify autonomous driving versus electrification, operational design domains and awkward literal expressions.
- Spanish: fourteen targeted corrections across AI companionship, anime, Beckham, Guinness, Saad Lamjarred and trucks. They distinguish a friend from the abstract relationship, preserve the source's parent/expertise/rest-limit meanings, make the absence of Guinness sponsorship explicit, and improve image descriptions.
- Seven broken external citation targets were repaired in all three languages. The exact original/replacement URLs and reasons are recorded in `tools/reviewed-citation-repairs.json`. Same-publisher replacements are explicitly distinguished from relocated pages. All seven final targets returned HTTP 200, and all 21 localized replacements were verified live. English source URLs were preserved as requested.
- The shared Arabic read-time label now follows Arabic number agreement: for example, `18 دقيقة قراءة` instead of `18 دقائق قراءة`. Reading-time values and the article layout are unchanged. Singular, dual, few, many and fallback cases have regression coverage; this label correction is included with the second-batch release.

## Verification of deployed corrections

- 174 indexable English/localized article pages and 354 missing-translation redirects: 528 HTTP checks passed.
- All 42 existing localized articles passed self-canonical, reciprocal hreflang, indexability, schema, image-alt and localized search checks; sitemap contains 196 URLs.
- All 3,327 first-batch translated body blocks and 129 FAQs were found in visible server-rendered HTML, excluding script data. All 72 inline internal links mapped correctly. Sitemap XML language alternates matched the page mappings.
- 80 desktop/mobile views covering all ten English originals and their three translations showed no horizontal overflow or clipped hero titles. French and Spanish shared component styles matched English. Arabic used RTL with right-aligned titles and body.
- Forty real browser language transitions across the ten articles reached the same article and showed exactly one active language. Keyboard opening and selection worked in the Arabic mobile dropdown.
- All 65 internal link destinations returned HTTP 200. Some external publishers denied, rate-limited or timed out automated requests; those responses were not treated as proof that a citation was broken.
- Production builds, TypeScript, lint and multilingual regression checks passed. No layout redesign or changes to the English originals were needed.
