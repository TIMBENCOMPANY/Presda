- Breguet's No. 160 and No. 1160 are separate pocket watches. Breguet's official re-edition history verifies 1783 order, 1827 completion and 2008 reconstruction presentation. It does not verify a $30m sale or dated appraisal. Both remain covered outside the ranked table.
- The recurring $26m Joaillerie 101 Manchette estimate was not authenticated by a dated primary valuation or sale. Current official Calibre 101 coverage does not prove that price. No claim that the Manchette was Queen Elizabeth II's coronation watch is made.
- Timeless Treasure's supported launch figure is $20m; no stronger evidence for $22m was found. Its official present price is on inquiry.
- Contemporary first-hand Billionaire reporting verifies a 2015 unveiling; later official Billionaire III history says 2016. Both are disclosed. Later Billionaire III specifications and images are not assigned to the original.
- Private sale invoices, current ownership and current asking prices are not inferred from old announcements. A sale not authenticated is not asserted never to have occurred.
