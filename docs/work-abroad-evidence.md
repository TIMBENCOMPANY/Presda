## Sources and status decisions

- Canada: IRCC Express Entry, FSW eligibility, accepted language tests, settlement funds, PNP directory, employer-specific permits, overseas applications and April 2026 fee changes. Job Bank's foreign-candidate page identifies postings open to international applicants. A profile, nomination or vacancy does not establish work authorization. PNP intake varies by stream.
- Germany: Make it in Germany's current Blue Card and Opportunity Card pages. 2026 gross annual Blue Card thresholds EUR 50,700 / 45,934.20 for eligible lower-threshold cases. Opportunity Card support EUR 1,091 net monthly; points-route language A1 German or B2 English; recognized-skilled-worker route differs. Secondary employment up to 20 hours weekly and trial employment up to two weeks per employer. Official Foreign Office mission guidance supports standard EUR 75 adult national-visa charge, subject to exceptions.
- European Commission and EURES: Blue Card operates in 25 EU states excluding Denmark and Ireland. EURES vacancies do not replace national immigration eligibility. National portals handle applications.
- France: France-Visas salaried-employment guidance, current visa wizard and application-entry page; France Travail job search. Previously broken wizard/application URLs replaced with the official live entry pages. Employer authorization and visa processing remain separate.
- Spain: current ministry initial employee-authorization instructions and SEPE official employment portal directory. Employer-led EX-03 procedure; labor-market conditions or exemptions; subsequent consular steps. An employment listing does not establish a permit.
- Netherlands: current IND highly skilled migrant guidance, 2026 income table, sponsor register and employer-facing Business Portal. Gross monthly salary excluding holiday allowance EUR 5,942 age 30+, EUR 4,357 under 30, EUR 3,122 only qualifying reduced-criterion cases. Standard application charge EUR 423. Register inclusion does not demonstrate a vacancy.
- Ireland: Immigration Service Delivery work-entry guidance and official Critical Skills permit information. Separate national system. No unsupported salary amount or intake claim.
- United States: official USCIS FY2027 bulletin establishes March 4 to March 19, 2026 initial H-1B registration window and USD 215 registration fee. This initial window is closed. State Department temporary-worker/immigrant-category guidance, fee table, Visa Bulletin, DS-160 and DOL SeasonalJobs provide the underlying route and job-order information.
- White House September 18, 2026 H-1B proclamation is described within its scope, exemptions and current implementation. The USD 100,000 condition is not a universal personal visa price. Proposed additional rules are not described as binding. Employer petition, selection, visa issuance and admission are distinct.
- State Department DV-2027 entry-period notice does not establish an open registration period. Current August 31 issuance guidance states issuance is paused while applications/interviews may continue. The March 11, 2026 Federal Register final rule supplies the passport/scan requirement with exemptions. No guessed autumn application dates or misleading open-entry link.
- Nationality-based U.S. visa restrictions and separate immigrant-visa issuance restrictions are linked separately. Category eligibility alone does not resolve nationality restrictions or exemptions.
- IRCC fraud guidance supports warnings about guarantees, false documents, impersonation and government lookalike domains. No route is sold as guaranteed employment, sponsorship or residence.
